Common questions

Drain Cleaning & Camera Inspection FAQs

These answers explain when professional attention or a closer inspection may be useful.

How do I know if I need drain cleaning?

Slow drainage, recurring clogs, backups and unusual drain activity can indicate that your drain needs professional attention.

What can a drain camera inspection find?

A camera inspection can help identify potential blockages, root intrusion, pipe damage and other issues inside the drain or sewer line.

Why does my drain keep clogging?

Recurring clogs can have several causes, including buildup, damaged pipes, roots or other obstructions.

Do you provide residential and commercial drain services?

Yes, SquadV provides drain services for residential and commercial properties in Edmonton and surrounding areas.

Do I need a camera inspection for every clogged drain?

Not necessarily. Camera inspection can be particularly useful when a blockage keeps returning or the cause is unclear.
