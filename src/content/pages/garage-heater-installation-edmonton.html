What affects the installation price?

The equipment and work needed for your garage affect the quote. Share your existing setup and ask the team to explain what is included in the proposed installation.

Which heater would suit my garage?

Start by sharing your garage dimensions, insulation details, and how you use the space. Discuss the available options with Squad V before choosing equipment.

What if my garage has no existing gas connection?

Mention this when you contact us. Squad V can discuss your existing setup and what needs to be assessed before confirming the installation scope.

What information should I send for a quote?

Include your location, approximate garage size, intended use, and whether you already have heating or a gas connection. You can still get in touch if you do not have every detail.
